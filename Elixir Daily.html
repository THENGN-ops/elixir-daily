Elixir Daily

How to play
  • Guess the card: you have 6 tries. Green means a match. An arrow points toward the right elixir cost or rarity.
  • Elixir count: the opponent starts with 5 elixir and gains 1 every 2.8 seconds, capped at 10. Subtract each card they play and say how much they have at the end.
  • Deck detective: one card is missing from a classic deck. Use the deck’s average elixir to work out which one.
  • Higher or lower: 5 pairs of cards that are 1 elixir apart. Pick the one that costs more.
  • Finish all four to fill your elixir bar and open the daily chest for Sparks and collectible titles. Play each day to build your streak.
